11 · ArabicThe whole app mirrors right-to-left, including the tab bar, icons and numbers. The language switch is on the sign-in screen and in Profile.
Removed from the app (they stay on the PC kiosk): chests, inventory, daily tasks, skins, clubs, voice calls, mini-games, store bundles. Profile (avatar → sheet): username, level, VIP, language, notifications, sign out, delete account. Delete account is required by Apple and Google. Push notifications: PC hold running low, PS session ending in 5 min, order ready, friend request, tournament starting.
